WHAT IT DOES

Built around the actual duty, not a generic checklist

Tap any card for the detail. Everything here is a real screen in the app, not a mockup.

PIN-gated field access

Section Commanders pick their Station and Rota, then enter a 4-digit duty PIN the Rota IC controls — no account to create, no password to forget mid-duty.

Details
Once unlocked, the device stays signed in for the day. The PIN is scoped per Rota, so Rota 1's commanders never see Rota 2's duty list, and the IC can change it anytime from Settings.

Defects need evidence

Every defect logged in the field requires exactly 2 photos before it can be saved — major defects get flagged with a visible warning throughout the whole review chain.

Details
A hydrant can carry up to 6 defects in one visit. The IC declares each one individually — major or minor — and once declared, it's locked permanently into the Overall Log.

Scheduling that matches a real rota

Master Schedule understands the 3-Rota rotation, suggests how many hydrants to assign per duty, and flags anything overdue from a past month separately from today's workload.

Details
Overdue means a hydrant's target month has passed and it still isn't complete — scheduling it doesn't clear the flag, only an actual completed inspection does.

Public and private hydrants, tracked differently

Private hydrants carry their own PUB submission checklist — the app reminds the IC once a hydrant's been scheduled but the submission to OwnselfGather hasn't been confirmed yet.

Details
It's a checklist, not an automation — the IC still submits the real form themselves, and just ticks it off here once it's done, so nothing is double-submitted or forgotten.

Reinspection, built into the lifecycle

A major defect sent to PUB for rectification can be marked fixed, scheduled for a recheck, and automatically closed out once the Section Commander confirms it on their next visit.

Details
Marking a hydrant rectified never silently changes its real completion status — the "Inspection completed" toggle stays off until the recheck genuinely happens.

One tap to WhatsApp

The IC can copy a full day's schedule or defect list straight into WhatsApp formatting — station, date, rota, grouped by appliance — without retyping a single hydrant number.

Details
The OIC gets a monthly summary version too — hydrants tested per Rota, major and minor defect counts, ready to paste into a station report.
