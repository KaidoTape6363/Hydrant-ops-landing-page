BEFORE YOU START

Pick your Station and Rota

No login needed — Field View only asks for where you're working. The Rota list only shows what actually exists for the Station you choose.

  • Tap Continue once both are selected.
  • Changing Station or Rota later means re-entering the duty PIN.

UNLOCK

Enter the duty PIN

Your Rota IC sets this PIN and can change it anytime from their Settings tab. Ask them if you don't have it.

  • Once unlocked, your device stays signed in for the rest of the day.
  • A wrong PIN just asks you to try again — nothing locks out.

YOUR DUTY LIST

Work through hydrants by appliance

Hydrants are grouped into tabs by appliance. Switch between List and Map view, or tap Plot Route to open Google Maps using your live location as the start.

  • Over 10 stops in one route gets split automatically — Maps can't route more at once.
  • A hydrant with no coordinates still shows in List view, just not on the map.

LOGGING A DEFECT

Two photos, every time

Select a defect from the Major or Minor list, then attach exactly 2 photos. "Inspection completed" stays locked until both are in.

  • Up to 6 defects per hydrant, each gets its own pair of photos.
  • Major defects show an amber warning icon through the whole review chain.

MASTER SCHEDULE

Assign the right number, not a guess

The quota card shows how many hydrants are due this month and suggests how many to assign per duty to stay on pace — scoped to today, not whatever's scheduled for later this month.

  • Filters collapse into one panel — tap Filters to reveal This month / All hydrants / Overdue, status, and search.
  • Overdue means a past month's target wasn't completed — scheduling alone doesn't clear it.

DEFECT LOG

Declare, then it's permanent

Every defect — major or minor — gets its own row with a two-step "Declare form submitted" confirm, gated by 2 photos, before it locks into the Overall Log.

  • Rectified major defects get their own section: mark rectified, then schedule a reinspection date.
  • A rectified-but-unreinspected hydrant won't silently show as completed.

OVERVIEW

Every KPI is clickable

Tap Pending, Overdue, or Open defects to open the exact filtered list behind that number — no separate screen to dig through.

  • Overdue uses the same definition as the Rota IC's view — past month, not completed.
  • All tallies start from October 2026 onward, excluding the app's September test period.

STATION-WIDE

See across all 3 Rotas at once

The OIC dashboard isn't scoped to one Rota — Overview, Defects Log, and Photo Gallery all span the whole station, so nothing requires checking three separate logins.
